# Bounded maturity assessment report

## Decision and assessment boundary

State the purpose, scope, period, model/source versions and whether this is self-assessment, facilitated work, a desk exercise or independently reviewed. Identify actual assessors/reviewers and authority. State readiness-only AI boundaries and exclusions. Do not imply certification or external endorsement.

## Four evidence views

Report authority/commitments, operated organisational capability, delivery/technical evaluation and actual human experience separately. Keep any legal/conformity decision in its own authorised record. Explain relationships without converting one into proof of another.

## Material barriers, uncertainty and participation

Include blocked tasks, residual consequences, unmanaged contradictions, missing evidence, unexamined services, denominators/context and method limits. Explain who influenced framing and decisions, what changed, dissent and missing perspectives. Protect personal information, small groups and quotations.

## Improvement and review

Link actual agreed actions/resources/owners and unapproved decisions. State the review point and material-change triggers, release/publication authority and accessible feedback route. Compare with previous scope/method carefully; no invented causal, representative, statistical, brand or ESG claim.
