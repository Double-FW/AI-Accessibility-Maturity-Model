## Updating a dependency

Record the old and new revision, changed guidance, affected criteria/records, assumptions and decisions needing review. Follow dependencies through design systems, suppliers and journeys. Reassess material conclusions; keep the previous report and its source baseline intact. Do not automatically replace a frozen historical RAG snapshot or silently rewrite its source IDs.

The method baseline is source commit `909cc7cf32bc4573f0844d054d5d4a37e5dda496`. This release adds an original assessment method; the baseline itself is not evidence of the method’s validation. The current repository revision used by a consumer must be recorded separately.
