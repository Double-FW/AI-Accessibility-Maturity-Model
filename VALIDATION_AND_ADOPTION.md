## Structural check scope

Local checks cover all six dimensions, all five levels and exactly one definition per AMM strand/cell; all applicable strand references and source IDs resolve; 30 cells contain dimension-specific capability, evidence, insufficient/contradictory evidence and reassessment fields; the complete fictional planning case references all 36 strands; 37 EX01 category routes resolve to local criteria; Markdown relative targets/anchors resolve where used; MIT and preserved historical source bodies are unchanged; current retrieval routes exclude historical duplicates.

These are document integrity checks, not execution of an assessment or tests of an operational organisation. There is no runtime/scoring engine, live integration or automatic policy enforcement. Checks cannot authenticate the truth of an evidence record or an approval. Do not describe these checks as the previous POURS repository’s test suite or claim product tests ran here.

## Independent calibration procedure

1. Recruit at least two suitably competent reviewers for a defined exercise; include varied relevant practice and accessible participation arrangements. Record roles, independence and conflicts. This is a planned exercise, not one already performed.
2. Give each the same versioned brief, criteria and fictional evidence independently. Ask for strand states, proposed cells/levels, uncertainty, exclusions, material barriers and evidence rationale before discussion.
3. Compare judgement and scope, not just headline numbers. Preserve every disagreement and the evidence or wording that caused it. Do not infer validity from simple agreement.
4. Have a competent adjudicator/owner review unresolved interpretations and explain changes. Update ambiguous criteria with a change record and repeat affected cases. Do not silently change results to force consistency.
5. Record recruitment/method, versions, coverage, individual interpretations, adjudication, limits and outstanding findings. Any reliability statistic requires an appropriate design/data and specialist interpretation; none is invented here.

Use all [worked cases](WORKED_ASSESSMENT_EXAMPLES.md), including missing evidence, blocked tasks hidden by averages, early participation, AI non-use, incomplete delivery assets, source/supplier change and unsupported external equivalence.

## Participant review and real pilot

Ask affected disabled practitioners/participants to review comprehension, navigation, burden, privacy and their ability to challenge conclusions and influence decisions. Offer accessible formats, pacing, breaks, support and fair participation terms. Do not require diagnosis or share identifying case material.

Pilot with a bounded organisational unit and real authorised owners. Record effort/capacity, source/evidence access, assessor competence, decisions and implementation/retest follow-through. Evaluate whether the method improves useful decisions, not merely the apparent level. Include unsuccessful/blocked stages and differing perspectives. Keep actual records in controlled systems.

## Adoption checklist

Before local operational adoption, confirm scope/roles/resources, criteria and source versions, data permissions, participation, independent challenge, evidence sampling, reporting/claim authority and correction/reassessment routes. Resolve material pilot findings or retain explicit limitations. Practitioner-pilot status is not general operational validation.

Before public claims, verify the exact model/source scope and evidence, actual approval, rights and privacy. No repository document alone grants the authority to certify, accept risk or make a legal conclusion. Record a new release/adoption decision with an actual owner and evidence when it occurs.
