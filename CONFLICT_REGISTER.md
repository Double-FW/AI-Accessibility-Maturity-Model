# Methodology and source conflict register

The decisions below are **Double FW method decisions specified for pilot**, not a claim of client adoption or approval by external publishers. Use Double FW requirements, methodologies, approaches and intended outcomes when they differ from external recommendations. Record the external requirement accurately; a local alternative does not fulfil its original wording automatically. Law/contract applicability remains a separate authorised decision.

## Standing decisions

| ID | Source/tension | Local decision and rationale | Affected criteria / review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CF01 | EX03 public guidance permits averages in some multi-area assessments and half-points | Keep scoped dimension/strand profiles, contradictions and limiting evidence. No averages or half-points; do not hide material exclusion. | All cells; review if a summary method is proposed |
| CF02 | A committee discussion might be treated as an unbiased result | Retain provenance, independent challenge, dissent and actual authority; agreement alone is not proof. | AMM-D1-05, AMM-D6-06; review method/source changes |
| CF03 | Original AI AMM level-5 language associates maturity with predictive AI use | Assess justified adoption, non-use, suspension and retirement; more AI is not greater maturity. Label readiness separately from deployed performance. | AMM-D5-01 through 06; change of AI use/scope |
| CF04 | Direct participation is most visible in the original highest-level narrative | Meaningful involvement starts with framing. Higher levels show sustained influence and governance continuity, not first participation. | AMM-D1-02, AMM-D2-06, AMM-D4-02; missing influence or changed participation |
| CF05 | Paperwork, course attendance, a scan or supplier declaration is treated as the outcome | Separate intent, operated capability, technical evidence and human outcomes; verify each relevant claim. | AMM-D2-01/02, AMM-D3-04/05, AMM-D4-05; unsupported claim |
| CF06 | Satisfaction or absence of complaints is treated as conformance or proof of access | Preserve independent findings; no complaints is not satisfaction, and a favourable account does not erase a failure. | AMM-D3-06, AMM-D4-05, AMM-D6-04; contradictory evidence |
| CF07 | All external models described as static compliance exercises | Position this as an AI-ready Double FW extension with accurate comparisons; W3C and AbilityNet already address organisational capability. | AI AMM / external register; new source evidence |
| CF08 | Different Double FW sources or historical/current copies disagree | Identify owning method, scope, revision, approval and authority. Do not silently select by date; record unresolved substantive differences for an actual owner. | All source-dependent assertions; material source change |

EX references resolve through [SOURCE_REGISTER.md](SOURCE_REGISTER.md); original model evidence is preserved in the dated historical RAG/source copies. CF03–CF08 are local interpretive controls, not assertions that every external publisher takes the rejected position.

- Double FW method preference; legal/contract applicability questions kept separate: [ ].
- Options, affected people’s input, risks, chosen interpretation and rationale: [ ].

Unresolved conflicts block the affected claim, not all unrelated useful work. A source preference does not authorise a person, allocate a budget, grant data access or confer legal approval.
