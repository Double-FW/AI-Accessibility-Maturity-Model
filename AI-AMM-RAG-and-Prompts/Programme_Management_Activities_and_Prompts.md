These eight activities and sixteen prompts support a human-managed assessment; they are not an executed assessment or authority to collect data or make decisions.

Replace bracketed inputs with actual authorised context. Request exact source IDs/files/sections/revisions, evidence IDs and limits. Distinguish guidance, adopted requirements, observed evidence, inference, proposals and unknowns. Keep personal records in approved systems. Real people retain participation, research validity, operational, legal and approval decisions; a generated role name is not confirmed authority.

## 1. Scope an assessment

**Runbook route:** A01. **Output:** A scoped brief, participation/capacity plan and open applicability questions.

### Example prompt 1

> Scope an assessment for [unit/services/employee and customer tasks]. Use the scope rules and A01. Confirm actual AI uses, decision owners, affected people, source versions, data limits and urgent barriers. Return the brief and unresolved decisions; do not infer an organisation-wide result.

### Example prompt 2

> Challenge [brief] for exclusions, unknown AI uses, missing receiving owners, late participation and unconfirmed authority. Propose precise changes and preserve unknowns.

## 2. Collect and map evidence

**Runbook route:** A02–A03. **Output:** Evidence requests and all applicable strand assertions with provenance and limits.

### Example prompt 1

> Use [authorised evidence] and A02–A03 to consider all 36 AMM strands. Record applicability, exact evidence/date/scope, observation, participant account, inference, contradictory sources and missingness separately. Planned work is not implemented practice.

### Example prompt 2

> Review [evidence profile] for duplicated snapshots counted as independent evidence, supplier claims treated as verified, private data exposure and missing actual task outcomes. Return corrected assertions and requests.

## 3. Interpret the 30 cells

**Runbook route:** A04. **Output:** A provisional six-dimension profile or Not determined with limiting evidence.

### Example prompt 1

> Apply the criteria to [evidence profile]. Examine each applicable strand and the relevant dimension cells with earlier positive controls. Show scope, evidence, contradictions and supported higher strengths. No averages, half-points or automatic legal conclusions.

### Example prompt 2

> Challenge [maturity claim] for missing evidence treated as Reactive, essential barriers hidden by an average, plans treated as operation and narrow samples described as whole-organisation maturity. Retain legitimate strengths and dependent unknowns.

## 4. Assess AI readiness and operation

**Runbook route:** A02–A05. **Output:** An AI-use/readiness boundary, capability findings and required evidence/actions.

### Example prompt 1

> Review [AI portfolio or verified non-use decision] against AMM-D5-01 through 06 and relevant other strands. Examine purpose, supplier/data, access/outcomes, oversight/permissions, changes, incidents and retirement. Label readiness-only scope and drills honestly.

### Example prompt 2

> Challenge [AI maturity report] for deployment volume used as maturity, simulated outcomes presented as live evidence, unsupported fairness/legal claims or inaccessible intervention. Propose evidence and decisions for actual owners.

## 5. Evaluate comparable experience

**Runbook route:** A02–A04. **Output:** A task/outcome measurement plan or bounded findings with missing voices retained.

### Example prompt 1

> Use the measurement guide for [tasks/decision]. Define correct outcomes, effort/waiting/recovery, chosen or forced help, choice, comprehension, dignity and satisfaction. Plan accessible participation and minimised data; preserve uncertainty and non-completion.

### Example prompt 2

> Review [dashboard or research summary] for missing denominators, inferred feelings/disability, unsupported representativeness/causality, zero complaints described as satisfaction and technical passes used as complete outcome evidence.

## 6. Plan delivery and improvement

**Runbook route:** A06. **Output:** An owned outcome-led plan with resources, dependencies and retest.

### Example prompt 1

> Turn [reviewed profile/barriers] into an improvement plan using the Double FW crosswalk. Include UX/brand systems, QA, supplier, support and employee/customer dependencies. Name owners/resources only when confirmed; prioritise actual impact, not score gain.

### Example prompt 2

> Challenge [roadmap] for unapproved commitments, training without applied capability, missing foundation/component assurance assets, urgent barriers postponed for workshops and corrective work closed without retest.

## 7. Review conflicts and claims

**Runbook route:** A05–A07. **Output:** A reasoned conflict/decision record and bounded report for authorised review.

### Example prompt 1

> Review [claims/source differences] against scope/authority, external coverage and CF decisions. Prefer Double FW methodology where external guidance differs, accurately describe the other model and preserve actual legal/contract questions separately.

### Example prompt 2

> Challenge [draft report] for score conversion, assumed endorsement, uninspected proprietary content marked covered, committee agreement called proof, unresolved internal Double FW conflicts or an invented approval. Return proposed wording and unresolved decisions.

## 8. Retest, report and reassess

**Runbook route:** A07–A08. **Output:** A period report, actual change/retest record and impact-based reassessment plan.

### Example prompt 1

> Compare [baseline and current evidence] with the same scope/criteria or explicit changes. Report each dimension, barriers, participant influence, dissent, implemented work, measured outcomes and remaining unknowns separately. Identify source/supplier/model/component change triggers.

### Example prompt 2

> Audit [closure or improvement claim] for altered scope hidden in comparisons, missing restored-task evidence, feedback silence inferred as satisfaction, invented pilot/calibration results and old RAG snapshots used as current authority.

## Human review contract

Before use, confirm the decision, scope, actual owners, source version, evidence/data permission and participation. After drafting, inspect claims, contradictions, source coverage, privacy and actual approval. No prompt grants authority to publish, certify, accept risk, spend funds or send communications. Check [validation/adoption status](../VALIDATION_AND_ADOPTION.md); independent calibration, participant review and a real pilot are not completed by generated output.
