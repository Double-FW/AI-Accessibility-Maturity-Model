## UX, branding and QA assurance

DF03-A owns the programme requirements for foundations, patterns/components, reference implementations and user-oriented ACT-style scripts. Apply that scope under AMM-D3-02 and actual execution under AMM-D3-04. Follow linked source methods for detailed techniques and tests. Evaluate configured content and complete journeys, not only a reference component.

Use DF06/DF07 for brand/CX and cognitive outcomes, DF08 for technical/evaluation identity, DF09 for task-level burden and DF10 for participation/data safeguards. One result may inform several assertions but must not be counted as independent evidence in each. Preserve best practice, local acceptance criteria, conformance requirements, actual results and user research as different things.

## Scope and source conflicts

Route specialist services onward to relevant public Double FW sources for chatbots, documents, media, charts, games or XR; not every organisation needs every product type. Do not substitute a private dependency for missing public guidance. Record an owned gap or obtain authorised access for non-public work without publishing the content.

Apply [scope and authority](MODEL_SCOPE_AND_AUTHORITY.md) and [conflict decisions](CONFLICT_REGISTER.md). Prefer Double FW methods over contradictory external recommendations while preserving actual obligations, accurate attribution, unresolved internal differences and the distinct adoption states of linked guidance.
