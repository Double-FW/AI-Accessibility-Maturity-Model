# External maturity-model coverage and limits

This register bounds comparisons with four named external models. It is not a claim to cover every maturity model, every questionnaire item or a proprietary assessment. Double FW retains its six dimensions/five levels; no score conversion, publisher endorsement or “W3C-compliant maturity” claim is made. Sources and access limits are recorded in [SOURCE_REGISTER.md](SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

## Relationship states

- **Local topic route specified:** the local criteria address this subject; no rating or complete external-item equivalence is implied.
- **Partial / alternative:** only part is adopted or a deliberate Double FW method differs; identify the difference and conflict record.
- **Not covered:** an inspected relevant item has no local response; assign work.
- **Not applicable with rationale:** justified for a particular assessment; never infer globally from one organisation.
- **Not verified:** source or instrument details were not inspected; do not mark covered or use them in a coverage percentage.

The rows below have topic/category scope. Before asserting complete external coverage, add an item-level record with exact source/version/locator, original item meaning, local strand/cell, relationship, evidence expectation, limitations, reviewer and decision. Preserve an explicit gap where rights or access prevents inspection.

## EX03 — Business Disability Forum public AMM guidance

The public guidance supports action-oriented self-assessment, stakeholder discussion and repeated review. Use AMM-D1-05, AMM-D4-06 and the assessment runbook for the local response. Its suggestion of averages/half-points in some contexts is a deliberate **Double FW alternative** under CF01; preserve scoped evidence instead. CF02 preserves challenge rather than assuming committee agreement proves accuracy.

The advertised 2026–2027 scorecard was not obtained as reviewable content through the inspected route. Individual checkpoints, full domain coverage and workbook scoring remain **not verified**. Do not invent those details or claim its benchmark equivalence.

## Review and claim record

Before a claim, record the named model/version, inspected extent, exact item, local response and status, source locator/rights, review owner/date and unresolved work. Recheck after source or local method changes. Use “maps the inspected topics/categories of the named sources, with gaps and alternatives disclosed” for the current documentation; do not claim complete item-level coverage, certification or external validation.
