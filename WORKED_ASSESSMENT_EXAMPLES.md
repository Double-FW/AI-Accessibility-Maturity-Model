# Worked assessment and challenge cases

All organisations, artefacts, people/roles, findings and decisions in this document are **fictional**. These are author-prepared desk cases to exercise the method, not actual assessments, human reviewer calibration, research findings or validation of real effectiveness. Independent assessors should first interpret the cases separately and then compare their reasons using [validation and adoption](VALIDATION_AND_ADOPTION.md).

## Case A — Complete planning-stage example

### A01 assessment brief

A fictional small organisation reviews one customer booking service, its support/complaints route and the employee work systems used to operate it. The purpose is to choose an improvement plan, not publish certification. Scope excludes unrelated services and no organisation-wide claim is made. A sponsor, assessment lead, functional owners, privacy adviser and external reviewer are proposed fictional roles. Their agreement exists only within the scenario. No real authority is asserted.

The fictional evidence period is one planning cycle. Current practice is uneven and a voice-only complaint route blocks some customers. No AI is deployed. The organisation is deciding whether a suggested AI support tool is appropriate. Accessible participation has influenced framing and the proposed decision; an input method and support choices are agreed in the fictional plan.

Capacity/date: to be confirmed in a real adoption. Intended evidence: actual non-voice reporting, accessible acknowledgement and human handover, retained context, correct case receipt, correction/retest and voluntary feedback. Ticket closure or non-response is not satisfaction. Reassess AMM-D3-06 and AMM-D6-04 after implementation and relevant channel changes.

## Case B — Absent evidence is not low maturity

**Input:** no training records are supplied; one team says work is informal, but no practice has been inspected. **Relevant strands:** AMM-D2-01/02. **Expected interpretation:** insufficient evidence for competence/repetition; Not determined if no cell is substantiated. Do not assign Reactive merely because documents are absent. Request proportionate practice evidence, not more paperwork as an end in itself. Independent review: pending.

## Case C — A strong average conceals an essential barrier

**Input:** several functions supply credible operational evidence, but an essential payment task and its complaint route remain blocked and unmanaged. A draft report averages all dimensions to Integrated. **Relevant strands:** AMM-D3-04/06, AMM-D4-05 and AMM-D6-04. **Expected interpretation:** reject the average and the dependent whole-scope claim, retain separate supported strengths, report the blocked scope and prioritise restoration/retest. Do not turn one failure into invented findings about unrelated governance evidence. Independent review: pending.

## Case D — Participation cannot start at level 5

**Input:** a project has passed internal reviews but participants were excluded from framing. The organisation says it will involve disabled people only after reaching Transformative. **Relevant strands:** AMM-D1-02, AMM-D2-06, AMM-D4-02. **Expected interpretation:** the participation-dependent cell is not supported. Obtain accessible input early enough to change scope and decisions; record missing perspectives and do not ask participants to rediscover a known barrier. Independent review: pending.

## Case E — Responsible AI non-use versus simulated operation

**Input:** an inventory and actual review find an AI product unsuitable; the organisation declines it. A safe oversight drill is planned but not run. A report claims proven live model fairness. **Relevant strands:** AMM-D5-01/03/04. **Expected interpretation:** non-use can support governance/readiness assertions in their scope, but a planned drill is not executed evidence and there is no live fairness result. Assess the applicable cells from actual evidence, mark deployment exclusions with rationale and withhold the false claim. Independent review: pending.

## Case F — Documented assets are not adopted delivery

**Input:** a component catalogue and brand guide exist. No configured consumer, manual/AT result, complete journey or source-change impact can be inspected. A scan is clean. **Relevant strands:** AMM-D3-02/04 and AMM-D4-05. **Expected interpretation:** only documentation is established; determine the supportable planning capability, keep actual adoption/outcomes not assessed, and request versioned configured evidence. The scan cannot verify brand meaning, cognition or whole-service access. Independent review: pending.

## Case G — Changed sources and supplier regression

**Input:** a model/renderer update breaks announcement order and changes the challenge route. The old release evidence is reused unchanged, and few complaints are described as satisfaction. **Relevant strands:** AMM-D5-05/06, AMM-D3-04/06, AMM-D4-05. **Expected interpretation:** open a change/incident record, identify affected tasks, restore access and reassess the dependent claims. Preserve the old baseline and uncertainty; silence is not a user finding. Independent review: pending.

## Case H — Conflicting or inaccessible external sources

**Input:** a comparison proposes a single numeric conversion from W3C and a proprietary workbook which has not been inspected; BDF-style half-points are used locally without explanation. **Expected interpretation:** no external score conversion is justified. Retain topic/category routes, mark the workbook not verified, use CF01 for the Double FW alternative and preserve accurate source descriptions. Do not use an internal method choice to waive law or claim publisher endorsement. Independent review: pending.

## Case I — Unmanaged contradiction versus well-managed incident

**Input:** a team operated recurring delivery controls, detected a consequential defect, informed affected people accessibly and restored the task with retest. A summary instead labels the entire organisation Reactive because any defect existed. **Expected interpretation:** assess the actual capability and scope; keep residual harm and test limits visible. A managed incident does not prove no maturity, and its recovery does not certify every service. Compare actual evidence to each applicable strand/cell. Independent review: pending.
