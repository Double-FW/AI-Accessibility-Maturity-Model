# Runbook: delivering a maturity assessment

Use this with [scope and authority](MODEL_SCOPE_AND_AUTHORITY.md), [criteria](ASSESSMENT_CRITERIA.md) and [templates](templates/README.md). It is a specified pilot method, not evidence of adoption or a completed assessment. Enter at an urgent barrier when necessary; do not wait for a maturity workshop to restore access.

## People and responsibilities to confirm

The sponsor confirms purpose, authority and capacity. The assessment lead maintains scope and evidence integrity. Function/service/supplier owners supply records and deliver changes. A research lead and affected people shape accessible participation and interpretation. Relevant accessibility, HR, privacy, risk and legal specialists verify matters within their competence. A reviewer challenges findings; the authorised decision owner approves the bounded report and actions.

These are responsibilities, not mandatory new job titles or committees. Confirm actual people, remit and availability in the brief. Document overlapping roles and obtain proportionate independent challenge for consequential claims. Champions support learning and handoffs; operational owners remain accountable. AI can draft and organise within permission but cannot provide independent participant evidence or approve its own assessment.

## A01 — Agree the assessment brief

**Inputs:** organisational facts, intended decision, existing obligations/commitments and known barriers. **Owner:** sponsor with lead and affected people.

Use [assessment brief](templates/assessment-brief.md). Identify unit, services, employee/customer tasks, regions, interfaces, suppliers/shared systems, actual AI uses and period. Establish baseline/source versions, scope exclusions, known legal questions, roles, capacity and a plan for accessible participation. Include non-users and people unable to start or complain. Agree communication, breaks, support and compensation where appropriate. Participation must influence framing, not just confirm an assessor’s view.

**Output:** confirmed scope and authority, open questions and immediate protective actions. **Gate:** do not make findings about excluded or unconfirmed scope. Missing legal interpretation blocks that conclusion, not unrelated authorised evidence work.

## A02 — Plan evidence and applicability

**Inputs:** brief and all 36 criterion strands. **Owner:** assessment lead with function owners and research/privacy specialists.

Create one record per strand and relevant scoped assertion in the [evidence register](templates/evidence-register.md). Mark applicable, not applicable with reason/reviewer/trigger, or applicability unresolved. Select evidence by consequence and coverage, including routine work, failures, variants, suppliers, support and changes. Specify dates, environments and the claimed period of repeatability. Agree interviews, observation and participant methods. Record known gaps; never silently exclude an ungoverned AI use or hard-to-access service.

Approve necessary data arrangements before collecting personal evidence. Consent to participate is not blanket permission to process or train models on data. Prefer minimised summaries and controlled evidence locators; do not copy sensitive records into this repository.

**Output:** evidence request list, sampling rationale and participation/data plan. **Gate:** proposed evidence is labelled planned, not observed.

## A03 — Collect and verify

**Inputs:** authorised sources and evidence plan. **Owner:** evidence contributors; assessment lead verifies provenance.

Inspect actual decisions, delivery records, staff practice, configured systems and relevant complete tasks. Check document ownership/adoption separately from practice. Verify the correct task end state, errors, assistance, recovery, waiting and abandonment. Test support and complaint routes as well as the product. Treat supplier declarations and self-assessments as claims requiring appropriate review. Ask participants open questions before brand/emotional prompts; preserve dissent.

Record method/version, source/date, scope, observer and limitations. Separate observation, participant account, inference and proposal. If a source cannot be inspected, use an evidence request or insufficient evidence; never generate plausible results.

**Output:** verified records and observed barriers, contradictions and missing evidence. **Gate:** evidence collection must not expose people to unsafe flashing, real harmful decisions or unauthorised consequential actions. Use safe fixtures and specialist methods.

## A04 — Assess strands and dimension cells

**Inputs:** evidence and [criteria](ASSESSMENT_CRITERIA.md). **Owner:** competent assessors with affected people’s input.

Apply each relevant AMM-Dn-xx strand and AMM-Dn-Lx cell. Use the stated assertion states. A provisional level needs the applicable strands and earlier positive capabilities supported in scope. Level 1 requires observed reactive practice, not absent paperwork. A claim contradicted by unmanaged material evidence is not supported. Show mixed subprofiles, higher strengths, residual barriers and Not determined where no level is fully evidenced.

For no deployed AI, retain actual non-use decisions and readiness evidence, justify deployment-specific exclusions, and label dimension 5 readiness only. A safe drill is not a live-system result. Never average the dimensions or infer conformity from a maturity level.

**Output:** proposed dimension profile and criterion decisions in [decision record](templates/decision-record.md). **Gate:** evidence links and uncertainty must be inspectable before a supported claim.

## A05 — Challenge and decide

**Inputs:** proposed profile, contradictions, participation record and material conflicts. **Owner:** reviewer and authorised decision owner.

Review whether evidence establishes operated capability rather than documentation alone. Check sampling gaps, hidden essential barriers, comparative claims, proxy measures, actual authority and evidence supporting every conclusion. Compare the profile with affected people’s accounts. Resolve methodological conflicts through [the conflict register](CONFLICT_REGISTER.md), preferring Double FW methods where appropriate. Keep law/contract interpretation separate.

Preserve different reviewer interpretations with their evidence and reasons. Change a finding only through a recorded decision, not consensus pressure. If a material issue cannot be resolved, publish the limitation or withhold the affected claim; continue useful correction work.

**Output:** bounded decision, dissent and unresolved actions. **Gate:** actual authority and claim scope are confirmed. A drafted approval field is not approval.

## A06 — Fund and sequence improvement

**Inputs:** profile and actual barriers. **Owner:** sponsor and receiving operational owners.

Use [improvement plan](templates/improvement-plan.md). Prioritise harm, exclusion, essential tasks, irreversibility, recurrence, scope and ineffective alternatives. Record dependencies, capacity, interim access, intended human outcomes, actual owners and success/retest evidence. Distinguish proposed allocations and dates from confirmed ones. Do not prioritise easy score increases over consequential barriers.

Use existing programme runbooks and systems through [the delivery crosswalk](DOUBLE_FW_DELIVERY_CROSSWALK.md). Link changes to foundations, components, suppliers and consuming journeys. Small organisations may combine roles while retaining explicit responsibility.

**Output:** owned/funded actions and unapproved resource decisions visibly open. **Gate:** a roadmap is not implemented improvement.

## A07 — Report and communicate

**Inputs:** reviewed profile and action decisions. **Owner:** reporting and approval owners.

Use [report template](templates/report.md). Give scope, period, sources, method, each dimension’s level or Not determined, evidence states, material barriers, uncertainty, dissent, participant influence and next decisions. Separate capability, technical findings, human outcomes and any authorised legal conclusions. State whether findings are self-assessed, facilitated, desk-exercise or independently reviewed.

Provide an accessible report and feedback route. Do not disclose personal evidence or small identifying groups. Any public comparison cites exact external model/version and the relationship, not an invented score equivalence or endorsement. ESG and brand claims need their own evidence.

**Output:** authorised report and communication record. **Gate:** an internal draft does not authorise external publication.

## A08 — Operate, retest and reassess

**Inputs:** agreed actions, actual changes, incidents, feedback and review triggers. **Owner:** service/function owners; lead maintains the assessment baseline.

Verify implemented changes and restored tasks, not ticket closure. Retain chosen/forced assistance and residual consequences. Reassess affected assertions after organisational, personnel, obligation, supplier, model, prompt, retrieval, component, content, permission, audience or method changes. Follow reverse dependencies and preserve the earlier report rather than silently replace it.

Invite voluntary feedback on remedy; silence is not satisfaction. Return learning to policy, design/brand systems, training and investment. Confirm a review point locally; no universal meeting cadence or assessment duration is imposed.

**Output:** retest, changed findings, source-impact record and next decision. **Gate:** report any regression or unresolved material contradiction without hiding it in an aggregate score.
